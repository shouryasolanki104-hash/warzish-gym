Our story

A club for every level

Warzish Fitness Club is a neighbourhood gym in Mahalaxmi Nagar, Indore, near the Bank of Baroda ATM. We offer a full strength floor, a cardio area, a Zumba area, a functional zone and a cafeteria, with coaches on hand. Our mission is to make regular training feel doable, whether you are lifting for the first time or chasing a personal best.

Gym Rules

These rules keep the club safe and pleasant for everyone.

On the floor

  • Wear proper training shoes and clean clothes.
  • Wipe machines after use and return weights to the rack.
  • Do not drop weights or slam machines.
  • Ask a coach if you are unsure how to use equipment.

Respect

  • Be considerate. Harassment, abuse or filming others without consent is not allowed.
  • Keep phone calls short and away from the training floor.

Belongings and hygiene

  • Use lockers where available and carry a towel.
  • Spitting, smoking and outside food on the floor are not allowed.

Last updated: November 2026. Questions: call 074155 66680.

Health and Safety

Your safety comes first.

Before you start

  • Speak to your doctor first if you have a heart condition, injury, pregnancy or other health concern.
  • Tell a coach about any injuries or limits.

While training

  • Warm up before lifting and cool down afterwards.
  • Use spotters or safety bars for heavy lifts. Increase weight gradually.
  • Stop and tell staff if you feel dizzy, short of breath or in pain.
